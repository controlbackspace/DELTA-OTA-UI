### Why cycles failed: the harness reset race

The updater starts an update on its own once the release is staged. The harness of Scenarios A and B then pulses the board's reset pin; if the update has already begun, the reset lands in the middle of the ota_0 -> ota_1 backup copy, the delta has no base and fails (`delta decode error`). `interrupted` = the serial log shows the update started and a reset line after it (function `update_interrupted`).
